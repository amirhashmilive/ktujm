Disclaimer, Neutrality & Content Policy

Official operational charter governing independent status, copyright compliance, and academic tone.

1. Statement of Independence

KTUJM Scholars (amirhashmilive.github.io/ktujm) is an independent, non-official educational resource and student mentorship initiative created by alumnus and Ph.D. scholar Amir Hashmi.

This website is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, authorized by, or endorsed by Kushabhau Thakre Patrakarita Avam Jansanchar Vishwavidyalaya (KTUJM), Raipur, Chhattisgarh. The official website of the university is hosted at https://www.ktujm.ac.in/.

2. Tentative Syllabus Notice

All curricula, paper codes, unit titles, and topic outlines published on this platform are reproduced tentatively by student editors for note-taking and revision structuring only.

University ordinances and syllabi are subject to statutory amendments by the Academic Council. For definitive evaluation schemes, pass marks, internal assessment criteria, and authoritative ordinances, students must always consult the official university website.

3. Institutional Neutrality & Tone Policy

To protect the academic reputation of participating scholars and maintain a welcoming space for all students, KTUJM Scholars enforces a strict institutional-neutrality policy:

  • Strictly Academic & Educational: Content is confined to subject notes, conceptual models, exam preparations, alumni career stories, and university-life resources.
  • Zero Campus Politics: Partisan political debates, union manifestos, personal allegations, administrative grievances, or protest campaigns are strictly prohibited.
  • Constructive & Supportive: Criticism must be confined to scholarly critiques of media theories and academic literature.

4. Copyright & Document Embedding Policy

In compliance with academic fair use and intellectual property respect:

  • We do NOT host, download, copy, or embed official university PDF documents, circulars, or proprietary question booklets.
  • All references to official statutory documents are provided exclusively as external hyperlinks to official university servers (ktujm.ac.in).
  • We do not utilize the official university crest, logo, or sealed emblem in our branding.
  • User-submitted notes are contributed under open academic sharing and remain the intellectual property of their respective student authors.
