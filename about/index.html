About KTUJM Scholars

An independent, permanent academic commons founded by alumni and run by Ph.D. scholars.

Founder's Vision

"University life moves fast. Every two or three years, brilliant research scholars write notes, compile model answers, mentor their juniors, and then graduate. When they leave, their accumulated wisdom and study materials often disappear with them, forcing the next generation of students to start from zero.

I founded KTUJM Scholars to create a permanent, open-access knowledge repository that never expires. By uniting Ph.D. scholars across departments into an organized, peer-review editorial collective, we ensure that every student—regardless of financial background or access to coaching—has free access to high-quality academic notes, exam guides, and senior mentorship."

— Amir Hashmi
Founder & Permanent Patron • Alumnus & Ph.D. Scholar

Core Pillars of the Platform

1. Free & Open Academic LMS

Every paper in every semester is broken down into structured, syllabus-aligned topics complete with exam-oriented 5-mark and 10-mark questions. No paywalls, no login barriers.

2. Permanent Ph.D. Scholar Oversight

Notes are not random crowdsourced snippets. They undergo rigorous verification by current doctoral researchers in Mass Communication, Journalism, Social Work, and Media Management.

3. Active Alumni Mentorship

Connecting current undergraduates with university graduates working in major newsrooms, public relations agencies, broadcast channels, and public policy institutions across India.

Institutional Neutrality & Disclaimers

KTUJM Scholars is an entirely independent, voluntary student initiative. It is not managed, funded, or administratively supervised by Kushabhau Thakre Patrakarita Avam Jansanchar Vishwavidyalaya.

  • We do not host or republish official marksheets, examination results, or confidential institutional documents.
  • We do not download or host copyright-protected university syllabus PDFs; all official ordinances are linked externally to ktujm.ac.in.
  • We maintain strict institutional neutrality and a constructive academic ethos free of campus politics.
