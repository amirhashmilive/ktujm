3-Tier Editorial Governance Model

Built to survive scholar turnover and graduation through systematic handovers and academic recognition.

Tier 1: Founder & Permanent Patron

Amir Hashmi (Alumnus & Ph.D. Scholar). Holds final vision, platform sustainability, alumni outreach, and senior appointments.

Tier 2: Managing Editor

1-year rotational term appointed from active senior Ph.D. scholars. Coordinates editorial workflow and calendar.

Tier 3: Departmental Coordinators

1–2 scholars per academic department. Responsible for syllabus fidelity, note verifications, and student submissions.
