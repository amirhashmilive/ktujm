Marketplace Code of Conduct & Policies

Non-Commercial Community Standards

  • Free / Cost-Recovery Only: Designed exclusively for textbook swaps, low-cost second-hand media books, and study material sharing. Commercial advertisements and third-party promotions are barred.
  • Lost & Found Board: An open directory to help students report and recover lost ID cards, notebooks, and equipment across campus buildings.
  • Zero Middleman Fees: KTUJM Scholars does not process payments or charge commission. All exchanges happen directly between enrolled students.
