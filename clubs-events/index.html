Active Campus Student Circles (Directory in Progress)

🎬 Film & Documentary Appreciation Club

Weekly screening and analysis of world cinema, Indian parallel cinema, and regional Chhattisgarhi documentaries in the Multi Media Centre.

📸 Lens & Shutter: Photography Society

Photo walks along the Kharun river banks, portraiture workshops, and annual campus photojournalism exhibitions.

🎙️ Radio Samvaad & Podcasting Group

Hands-on voice modulation, audio storytelling, scriptwriting, and community radio broadcasting sessions.

✍️ Madhavrao Sapre Literary & Debate Society

Debates on media ethics, press freedom discussions, essay competitions, and creative writing workshops in Hindi and English.
