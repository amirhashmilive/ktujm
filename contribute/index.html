How to Contribute

Join our student-scholar network. Submit lecture notes, refine existing stubs, or interview prominent alumni.

Method 1: Direct GitHub Pull Request

For students and scholars familiar with Markdown or GitHub:

  1. Fork the repository at amirhashmilive/ktujm.
  2. Locate the relevant topic stub under content/notes/<course>/sem-<n>/...
  3. Replace the placeholder text with your definitions, concepts, and diagrams.
  4. Update the frontmatter: change status: "draft" to "review" and add your name.
  5. Submit a Pull Request. A Ph.D. Coordinator will review and merge it.
Open GitHub Repository ↗

Method 2: Email / GitHub Issue Submission

For students who prefer writing in Word, Google Docs, or plain text:

  1. Structure your notes according to our standardized 7-section template.
  2. Include course name, semester, paper code, and unit number.
  3. Attach your document in a GitHub Issue or send it to your Departmental Coordinator.
  4. A scholar editor will format it in Markdown, attribute your authorship, and publish it.
Submit via GitHub Issue ↗

Standardized 7-Section Note Template

All published notes adhere to this uniform pedagogical structure:

1. Definition
Precise, academic definition with authoritative citations (e.g., Wilbur Schramm, Denis McQuail).
2. Key Concepts & Models
Core principles, theoretical frameworks, and bulleted breakdowns.
3. Diagram (Mermaid / Flowchart)
Visual depiction of the process, hierarchy, or model relations.
4. Examples (Chhattisgarh / India Context)
Real-world media campaigns, state public relations instances, or regional case studies.
5. Exam-Style Questions
Short answer (≤100 words, 5 marks) and long comprehensive questions (10 marks).
6. Further Reading
Standard reference textbooks and research papers.
7. References
Full bibliography in APA or MLA format.

Alumni Interview Guide (Standard 8–10 Questions)

Scholars conducting interviews for our Career & Alumni Spotlight should use this standardized question set:

  1. Academic Journey: Which course and batch did you graduate from at the university, and what drew you to this field?
  2. Campus Memories: Looking back at your time at Kathadih campus, what is your most defining classroom, studio, or field memory?
  3. Career Turning Point: What was your first professional breakthrough after graduation, and how did your university education prepare you for it?
  4. Current Role: Could you describe your current responsibilities and the most exciting project you are leading today?
  5. Industry Realities: How has the media/communication landscape changed since you were a student, especially with digital tools and AI?
  6. Crucial Skills: What specific skills (technical, analytical, or interpersonal) should current students cultivate to succeed in the industry?
  7. Advice on Internship & Portfolio: How should students approach building a professional portfolio during their degree?
  8. Overcoming Setbacks: What was one significant professional hurdle you encountered, and how did you navigate it?
  9. Giving Back: In what ways can alumni contribute to mentoring the next generation of students?
  10. Message to Scholars: What is your parting message for current students and researchers stepping into the field?

Academic Tone & Quality Standards

Non-Negotiable Quality Rules

  • No Plagiarism: Do not copy-paste directly from Wikipedia, commercial study guides, or copyrighted books. Synthesize in your own words.
  • Institutional Neutrality: All content must remain strictly educational, objective, and scholarly. Campus politics, personal grievances, and partisan debates are prohibited.
  • Proper Attributions: Quote sources, authors, and years accurately.
